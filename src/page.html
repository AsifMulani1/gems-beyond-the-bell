One programme,
many possibilities

The proposition and the identity stay the same wherever you are in GEMS India. What is actually on offer does not.

Every school curates its own list around what its children ask for, the facilities it has, and the coaches and partners it works with. Some campuses run academies with specialist partners; at others our own coaches lead the sessions.

So this page tells you what Beyond the Bell is. Your school's page tells you what is running, when, what it costs and how to join.
